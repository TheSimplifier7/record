How the count is kept

By week, the count this page keeps.

By level, the count that flatters it less.

The words. Held: the close stayed on the right side of the named line. Hit: it also went through the next level named on that side; a hit counts once, as held, and is marked. Wrong: the close crossed the line; the row stays, struck through, for good. Never reached: price never came to the line, so the week proves nothing and counts for neither side. Open: named, with the clock still running, counted for neither side until the close that grades it. Every row from 28 September also says which way it reads, holding above its line or staying below it, the next level on that side, and the chance the line holds, as printed the day it was named.

One rule. Held if a weekly close on the target's side of it prints on or before the last weekly close inside the window; wrong if the window closes without one. A range is graded on its nearer bound. Averages, "toward", "approach" and claims with no window are not targets and are not on the board; they are listed by name, with the reason, on the full board, with every quote. Every close on this board is read off this desk's own chart; where one is not yet, the row says as reported and the figure is replaced when it is read. A grade never turns on the decimals.

How a daily level is graded

Each level is a floor or a ceiling, and each has one Exit: a daily close through it. A floor exits on a close below it. A ceiling exits on a close above it. A close exactly on the level counts as held. Held means the day closed on the side I named; it is not a trade result. The close is 17:00 New York, 23:00 Madrid in most weeks, read off my own chart.

The 08:00 post on X is the original. This page copies it into daily.json, and every change to that file is a public commit. If this page and the post ever differ, the post stands and the difference is logged here as a correction. A trading day with nothing on file is shown as not on file, never left out.

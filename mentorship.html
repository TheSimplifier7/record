The record1-to-1 mentorship

Learn to build and check your own levels

Twelve weeks of 1-to-1 work on gold, silver and platinum. You practise naming a level in advance, deciding what would change your view, and checking the result in your own record.

This is personal teaching, not the monthly indicator subscription. The price is given on the call and put in writing before payment.

What the twelve weeks involve

Twenty-four 60-minute sessions, two a week. You use your own TradingView chart. You name your own levels and review them against Friday's close. The aim is a process you can keep using and a record you can keep checking.

You make your own decisions and use your own account. No one handles your funds or tells you what to buy or sell.

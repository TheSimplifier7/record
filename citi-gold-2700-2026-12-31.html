How every target is graded

In plain words: a target is held if price closes a week at the target or beyond it before its deadline. If the deadline passes without that close, it is wrong.

The one rule. Held if a weekly close on the target's side of it, at or above for a target above price, at or below for a target below, prints on or before the last weekly close inside the window. Wrong if the window closes without one. A range is graded on its nearer bound.

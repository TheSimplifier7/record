A method, taught. A ledger, graded. Nothing managed, nothing advised, nothing sold that you would have to disclose.

What the seat is

Twelve weeks, one to one, twice a week. Three books, position, swing and tactical, with the fastest one held back until the slower two are graded. The client runs the desk's own indicator on his own chart and is taught, from past tape, where the conditions sat for a long or a short and what the close did afterwards. He then names his own level, with his own kill, and trades it live, on his own account, on his own decision. He keeps a ledger, graded on Friday's close by the same rule as the public record, and he keeps it after.

What it is not
Education and training. No investment advice. No personal recommendation on any instrument. No discretionary or advisory management. No funds handled, pooled, or shared in. No signal service. No income claim and no guarantee of any result, to him or to you. Any client who wants to be told what to buy is told no, and sent back to you.
What saying the name costs you
  1. Nothing changes hands. No fee, no share, no favour, in either direction, ever. There is nothing to disclose because there is nothing.
  2. The introduction is yours as a person who knows the work, not your firm's. The desk never uses your name or your firm's, anywhere.
  3. The client is sent one message, the record link and two sentences, and offered a thirty-minute conversation. He is not chased. One follow up, then silence.
  4. Terms are in writing, signed before payment, and state everything above. He can show them to you.
  5. If he ever asks the desk what to do with money you manage, the answer is that it is yours to manage, and he is sent back to you.
What your client says afterwards

The first client in the seat has said, in his own words, what it has been for him. Those words, dated and kept, are available on request once he has approved them for use, without his name.

If a client of yours asks, give him the name and nothing else. The desk will send him the record. Everything after that is between the two of them, and it is written down.

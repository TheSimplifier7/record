The backstory

UBS's first number for 2026. On 29 December 2025, with gold at 4,322.40 as reported, it said 5,000 by September 2026, and 5,400 if the US midterm elections brought political or economic turmoil. The same note saw a pull back to 4,800 by the end of 2026.

Only the first clause carries a level, a window and one direction, so only it is on the board. The 5,400 is a condition and the 4,800 is the return leg of a path; both are listed below with their reasons.

How every row is graded

The one rule. Held if a weekly close on the target's side of it, at or above for a target above price, at or below for a target below, prints on or before the last weekly close inside the window. Wrong if the window closes without one. A range is graded on its nearer bound. Graded on the TVC weekly close, read off this desk's own chart, the same close every row on the record is graded on; where a close predates this desk's own reads the row says as reported, and a grade never turns on the decimals.

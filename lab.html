How a tool gets made

One rule: nothing reaches my desk until it has earned it.

  1. The idea

    It starts with what traders actually use and argue about. If it has been done well already, I am not making another copy. If it has been done badly, that is the opening.

  2. The build

    Before a line of code, the idea gets written up properly, with its sources. No write-up, no code.

  3. The beating

    Every build runs the six checks below. Fail one and it goes back. No exceptions for ideas I happen to like.

  4. My desk

    If it survives all that, it goes on my own gold charts and I read the market with it the way I do every day. Plenty die here too.

The six checks

All six, every time. Fixes to a tool already on your chart run checks 2 to 4 again.

  1. Sourced

    Every idea has to stand on something real.

  2. Clean

    It loads fast and does not choke your chart.

  3. No repaint

    If it rewrites history after the fact, it is lying to you, and it is out.

  4. Chart-friendly

    Your candles stay the star. Nothing on it gets in the way of reading price.

  5. Proven on gold

    It has to earn its place on my own gold charts.

  6. The bar

    It has to stand next to the best of what I already have. Fine is not finished.

How a concept gets counted

The tools are half the lab. The other half is testing what the crowd swears by. Same discipline, written down before anyone looks at the data.

  1. The rules come first

    The test is written down before anyone looks at the data. One run, no second tries.

  2. A fair fight

    Every concept goes up against something dumb. If it cannot beat that, it has nothing.

  3. A bar to clear

    Close is not enough. The bar is set before the run, costs paid. Miss it and it does not hold.

  4. Years it never saw

    Some of gold's history is locked away. Nothing becomes a tool until it holds there too.

  5. Checked again every month

    Anything that held runs again on new data. If it flips, you see the flip.

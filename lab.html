Does not hold

Does a squeeze on gold come before a bigger move?

No. After a squeeze, the next 12 candles moved less than they do after an ordinary stretch at the same hour of the day, on both clocks and on both stretches of years. Measured in ATRs, every clock reads the move after a squeeze bigger, 1.05 to 1.14 times an ordinary stretch: the ATR shrinks while price is quiet, so any move after it looks big.

A squeeze: the last 12 candles' range in the lowest tenth of the past year's on that clock. 5 minute gold: 486 squeezes from 1 Jul 2025 to 2 Nov 2026, 3,210 from 2 Jan 2020 to 30 Jun 2025. 40 minute gold: 76 and 559. Squeeze against ordinary stretch: 0.58 to 0.73 on the hour's usual range, 1.05 to 1.14 in ATRs. On the lab's standing protocol three cells fail and 40 minutes since July 2025 is too few to say (76).

From the deskEvery desk has someone sitting on a quiet market, waiting for it to explode. Most days it stays quiet and he pays for the wait. On gold, quiet came before quiet.

Do swing highs and lows pull price to them?

No. Price reached the nearest untouched swing high or low as often as any level the same distance away, and a swept one turned back no more often than any other high or low.

5 minute gold, 2 Jan 2020 to 30 Jun 2025: 28,613 swing highs and 29,683 swing lows. The same answer from 1 Jul 2025 to 30 Oct 2026.

From the deskOn a bank desk you see the orders, not the lines on a chart. The orders sit where people need to trade, and they move every day. A swing high is a line you drew. Gold has no idea it is there.

Does not hold

Does a channel's 2 deviation wall hold?

Not as promised. Carried ten candles on, one close in three went beyond it, against one in twenty promised, and more than twice as often against the run as with it.

5 minute gold, the line over 50 closes, 2 Jan 2020 to 30 Jun 2025: 8,081 channels. From 1 Jul 2025 to 30 Oct 2026: 33.2 beyond.

From the deskA channel is the last fifty closes drawn neatly. The wall promises one close in twenty beyond it and gold gives you one in three. Respect the close, not the line someone fitted to yesterday.

Does not hold, against gold's own candles shuffled

Does the slow half of a trend read the day better than the fast half?

No. It matched legs about a day long 69.9 and 69.3 times in 100, but on the same candles shuffled inside each day it matched them 72.8 and 72.2 times on average over 200 shuffles: inside the day, gold's candle order gave it nothing over random order. Read forward, its side matched the next 276 candles' move 50.2 and 50.1 times in 100; saying up every time matched 52.7 and 53.7.

5 minute gold, chosen on 1 Jul 2025 to 2 Nov 2026: 325 day-long legs. Checked on 2 Jan 2020 to 30 Jun 2025: 1,348. 200 shuffled copies of each. As I first filed it, the slow half beat the fast half 69.9 to 67.5 and 69.3 to 66.7; the shuffled candles show a gap as big.

The Flow keeps the slow half for what it does: it changes colour 4.3 times a day against the fast half's 9.7 to 9.8. That describes, it does not predict.

From the deskI built the slow line, then had my own count of it run against gold's candles shuffled. The shuffled candles scored higher, so it reads the day's legs no better than shuffled candles do. What it does do is change its mind less than half as often as the fast line, and every change of mind costs you a spread and a decision.

A lead, not yet a sealed test

Does a turn pay more with every bigger clock behind it?

On 5 minute gold since July 2014, 11,169 turns with the 23 hour, 8 hour, 2 hour and 40 minute clocks all behind them made 0.140 of their risk a turn, and were ahead in each of three stretches of years. Turns against the day's clock lost 0.153.

The turn: the chart's own 40 candle average changing direction. 5 minute gold, July 2014 to 2 Nov 2026.

Shipped in The Gate: it marks only the turns the whole stack backs.

From the deskThe trades worth sizing up had the whole day behind them, not just the last hour. One clock saying go is an opinion. Every clock saying go is the market agreeing with you. It stays a lead until the sealed test is in, and the card says so.

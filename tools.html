The WeekThe Week: floors and ceilings, counted

Last week's high is this week's ceiling. Last week's low is this week's floor. The Week draws both, with last week's close, and counts how often each one has held on the chart you load it on.

The Week on TradingView

The ClockfaceThe Clockface: is the day's high in yet

Is today's high already in? The low? The Clockface puts odds on both three times a day, grades every one at the day's close on the chart you load it on, and prints beside each how often odds like it came true on that chart.

The Clockface on TradingView

The AtlasThe Atlas: odds of a touch before the close

Which of today's levels can price still reach before the close, and at what odds? The Atlas draws today's levels and puts one figure on each: the odds of a touch before 17:00 New York, as graded on the chart you load it on.

The Atlas on TradingView

AMA DashboardAMA Dashboard: Flow and Structure

AMA Dashboard reads the trend from two adaptive moving averages and puts the chart into plain words: which side price is on, how steep the move is, how far it has run, and one grade.

AMA Dashboard on TradingView

Gold's SessionsGold's Sessions: each session against its usual range

Gold's Sessions draws gold's three sessions, Asia, London and New York, each as a shaded band from its high to its low across its hours, and around each of today's sessions a dotted frame one usual range tall, set on its open: its top half the usual over the open, its bottom half under it. A band inside its frame is a smaller session than usual; one that breaks through it, a bigger one. A label under each session and a panel set each of today's sessions against its usual: 1.52 times usual means the session ran half as far again as it usually does.

Gold's Sessions on TradingView

The Stop OddsThe Stop Odds: how often a stop this far away was hit at this hour

The Stop Odds shows where a stop would be hit, and how often, over the window you choose: the next two hours, the next four, or to gold's 17:00 New York close.

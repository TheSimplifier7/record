Notes on the standard
01

Standard tightened 3 July 2026. Every claim now ships with the condition that would grade it a miss; untested levels are stated as no test rather than counted as wins; partial grades count against the record in any ratio quoted. Earlier entries were graded under the prior convention, are marked pre-standard in the ledger, and are preserved unaltered. No grade is ever restamped to fit a rulebook written after it. Rows carrying no published kill are marked as such.

02

Correction, 18 July 2026. The Verdict posted 17 July graded the 12 July gold floor claim a pass. The published kill, a weekly close beneath 4,098, had in fact fired at 4,018.44. The correct grade is a miss and the ledger carries it. A third claim from the same map, the stair at 4,305.32, went ungraded in that Verdict and is stated here as no test. The erroneous grades, this correction, and the grading process failure behind them are all logged and all remain.

03

Correction, 30 July 2026. The Updated field on this page was rendered from the visitor's own device clock rather than from the file. While that line was live, the page told every reader it had been updated on the day they opened it, whatever the file's actual state. No call, no grade and no figure in the ledger was affected. The field is now set by hand against the commit. The fault is logged here rather than fixed quietly, because a page that claims nothing is hidden does not get to hide this.

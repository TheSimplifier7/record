The words. Held: the close stayed on the right side of the named line. Hit: it also went through the next level named on that side; a hit counts once, as held, and is marked. Wrong: the close crossed the line; the row stays, struck through, for good. Never reached: price never came to the line, so the week proves nothing and counts for neither side. One rule. Held if a weekly close on the target's side of it prints on or before the last weekly close inside the window; wrong if the window closes without one. A range is graded on its nearer bound. THE RUNNING SCORE

How the week's odds move

Each line is named on Monday with the chance it holds at Friday's close, read off the founder's chart, and that figure never changes. After each close from Tuesday to Thursday the same chart is read again on the 8h bar that closes at 23:00 Madrid time: the close, and the odds it prints of a weekly close above and below the week's range. They go into the day's line as printed, with a snapshot of the chart beside them. The range runs from the line's next level to the line, so one of the two is the odds of a close through the line, and the chance it holds is 100 less that figure. A reading is never edited; a day with no reading says so. Friday's close grades the line, with the week's readings beside it.

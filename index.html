Standing Calls

The direction, and what would end it

In plain words: gold has been bullish on this page since the 7 August close, silver since 21 August, platinum since 19 August, and the year-to-year call has been bullish since 7 June. Each card below names the exact line that ends it.

Graded on their own clocks against a named close, never restamped, and never counted in the tally above. When one ends it stays on this page with the date it ended and what ended it, and when one metal inside a two-metal call ends, the end is written into the call and the call stays. Gold has been bearish since 13 March. That structure ended on the 7 August close, the swing clock turned bullish the same night, and it is bullish still. Silver turned bullish on the 21 August close and closed under its line on 28 August without the structure turning, so the claim stands: from 29 August this page dates a swing turn by the epoch midline crossing the slow mean reversion line, never by a price close, which is the standard the secular clock has run on since 1 August. The weekly rows keep their own published price kills and are graded on them, which is why the silver row of 23 August grades wrong and stays. Platinum's first row on this page passed at the same close and on the same rule, and its swing clock runs from the 19 August close. See notes 06, 08, 10 and 11.

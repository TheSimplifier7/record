Five questions

What do I get free?

Three levels every trading day, one each for gold, silver and platinum, posted on X at 08:00 CET, and the close that graded each one. Seven open-source TradingView tools are free too.

When are the levels posted?

At 08:00 CET on every trading day. In most weeks that is 02:00 in New York and 07:00 in London.

How is a level graded?

Each level is a floor or a ceiling, and each has one Exit: a daily close through it. A floor exits on a close below it. A ceiling exits on a close above it. A close exactly on the level counts as held. Held means the day closed on the side I named; it is not a trade result. The close is 17:00 New York, 23:00 Madrid in most weeks, read off my own chart.

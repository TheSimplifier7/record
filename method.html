Before you start

You are buying a charting-tool licence, not personal advice or buy-and-sell instructions.

What is an indicator?

It is a tool that adds information to a price chart. These tools draw reference lines and the wider price pattern. They do not place trades for you.

Do I need TradingView?

Yes, for The Fan or The Method. You need your own TradingView account. The free plan can run either tool, subject to its limit on indicators per chart. The daily levels on X do not need TradingView.

What does "2-hour view on a 10-minute chart" mean?

Each bar on a 10-minute chart covers ten minutes of prices. The Fan also shows the wider 2-hour view on that chart, so you can see shorter moves against the wider pattern.

Should I choose The Fan or The Method?

Choose The Fan if you want the wider view while looking at shorter moves. It includes The Method. Choose The Method if the simpler reference line is enough. Neither tool makes the decision for you.

How does the free trial work?

Either plan starts with 7 days free. A card is required, but nothing is charged during the trial. Cancel before day 8 and pay nothing. Otherwise, Stripe charges €149.99 for The Fan or €59 for The Method on day 8, then every month until you cancel. Prices include VAT. Check the exact first-charge date in checkout.

Why is a card required?

So the subscription can continue after the trial without another checkout. Stripe handles your card details. I never see your full card number. You choose whether to keep it by cancelling before day 8 if it is not for you.

How do I cancel?

Use Manage or cancel your subscription in the website footer, or the billing link in your Stripe receipt. Follow the steps until cancellation is confirmed and keep the confirmation. You can also email hello@thesimplifier7.com. Cancel before day 8 to pay nothing. Removing the indicator from your chart does not cancel billing.

What happens after I cancel?

If you cancel during the free trial, you pay nothing and access ends when the trial ends. After a paid month has started, cancellation stops renewal and access continues until the end of the period you paid for. Your separate withdrawal rights are explained in the Terms.

When do I get access?

Within an hour of checkout, or by 09:00 Europe/Madrid if you join overnight. Access is attached to the exact TradingView username you give, not your password. Find it under Indicators, then Invite-only. If the tool is missing, refresh and check the username first.

Does it tell me when to buy or sell?

No. It shows information on your chart. Whether to trade, how much risk to take and when to stay out are your decisions. You do not need to place a trade to try the display.

Is this financial advice?

No. The tools, levels and record are for education and information. They do not consider your finances or give a personal recommendation. Trading can lose money. Past results do not guarantee future results, and no profit or income is promised.

Can I see the calls that were wrong?

Yes. Open Read every wrong call. Each call is graded on its close, and the wrong calls stay public. A call marked held is not a claim that a trade made money.

What if I change plans?

The current terms allow a move from The Method to The Fan through the billing page, paying the difference. If you are still in the trial, the trial continues. Read the price and date shown before confirming a change.

Do I need experience?

You should understand a price chart and be able to make your own trading decisions before paying for a tool. If you are new, start with the free daily levels and read how each call is checked. The tool does not make trading safe.

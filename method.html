One question each

They are built to read together on one chart, and each one says what it will not do.

The Fan

Which side of the 2 hour mean is price on, and did the last test of the mean hold?

It says where price stands against the mean. It does not say where price goes next.

The Keel

Which way does the flow run, and what exact close would end it?

It describes the flow. It does not predict the next move.

The Method

How far does price stand from the mean, and how does this leg compare with every gold leg since 2014?

A leg that has run far is no more due to come back than one that has not.

The Hinge

Is the swing confirmed, did the last one hold, and where is the structure?

It confirms swings earlier. It is not right more often.

Gold, 1 hour, each as TradingView drew it on Sunday 15 November 2026, with the week closed. A past chart, not a live trade.

Before you start

You are buying a charting-tool licence, not personal advice or buy-and-sell instructions.

What is an indicator?

It is a tool that adds information to a price chart. These tools draw reference lines and the wider price pattern. They do not place trades for you.

Do I need TradingView?

Yes, for the four tools. You need your own TradingView account. The free plan can run them, subject to its limit on indicators per chart. The daily levels on X do not need TradingView.

New to TradingView?

The four tools, and every free one I publish, run on TradingView. If you do not have an account yet, open one through my link. The Basic plan is free. TradingView says that once you buy your first paid plan through a partner link, you get $15 off your next one.

What does "2-hour view on a 10-minute chart" mean?

Each bar on a 10-minute chart covers ten minutes of prices. The Fan also shows the wider 2-hour view on that chart, so you can see shorter moves against the wider pattern.

What do I get?

All four: The Fan, The Keel, The Method and The Hinge, added to the TradingView username you give at checkout. One subscription, €99 a month including VAT. Neither the tools nor I make the decision for you.

How does the free trial work?

The set starts with 7 days free. A card is required, but nothing is charged during the trial. Cancel before day 8 and pay nothing. Otherwise, Stripe charges €99 including VAT on day 8, then every month until you cancel.

Why is a card required?

So the subscription can continue after the trial without another checkout. Stripe handles your card details. I never see your full card number. You choose whether to keep it by cancelling before day 8 if it is not for you.

How do I cancel?

Use Manage or cancel your subscription in the website footer, or the billing link in your Stripe receipt. Follow the steps until cancellation is confirmed and keep the confirmation. You can also email hello@thesimplifier7.com. Cancel before day 8 to pay nothing. Removing the indicator from your chart does not cancel billing.

What happens after I cancel?

If you cancel during the free trial, you pay nothing and access ends when the trial ends. After a paid month has started, cancellation stops renewal and access continues until the end of the period you paid for. Your separate withdrawal rights are explained in the Terms.

When do I get access?

Within an hour of checkout, or by 09:00 Europe/Madrid if you join overnight. Access is attached to the exact TradingView username you give, not your password. Find it under Indicators, then Invite-only. If the tool is missing, refresh and check the username first.

Does it tell me when to buy or sell?

No. It shows information on your chart. Whether to trade, how much risk to take and when to stay out are your decisions. You do not need to place a trade to try the display.

Is this financial advice?

No. The tools, levels and record are for education and information. They do not consider your finances or give a personal recommendation. Trading can lose money. Past results do not guarantee future results, and no profit or income is promised.

Can I see the levels that were wrong?

Yes. Open Read every wrong level. Each level is graded on its close, and the wrong ones stay public. A level marked held is not a claim that a trade made money.

Do I need experience?

You should understand a price chart and be able to make your own trading decisions before paying for a tool. If you are new, start with the free daily levels and read how each level is checked. The tool does not make trading safe.

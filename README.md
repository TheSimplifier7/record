# record

The public track record of THE SIMPLIFIER. Gold and silver.

**Live record: https://thesimplifier7.github.io/record/**

Every call is named before the move and graded after. The wins and the misses sit in the same place. Nothing is ever removed.

The file only grows. The commit history of this repository is the proof. If a call was wrong, it is still here.

## How a call is graded

Every claim is published with the exact close that would prove it wrong. That number goes out with the call, before the move.

On Friday at the weekly close, every open claim is graded in public. Right, wrong, or never reached.

A level that was never reached is marked no test. It counts for neither side. It is never banked as a win.

The page states counts only. It does not publish a strike rate, because a percentage on a small base becomes an argument about the denominator instead of a record. The rate is easy to work out. A reader who does the division will find the count was already honest.

Grades are not restamped to fit a rulebook written after them. The standard tightened on 3 July 2026. Earlier entries are marked pre-standard and left exactly as they were graded.

Corrections are published on the page as numbered notes. That includes the corrections against me.

## Cadence

A call is named on Sunday, before the market opens. It is graded on Friday, at the weekly close.

Both at 23:00 CET. The Friday grade goes out at 23:15, because the weekly close prints at 23:00 and nothing is graded before the candle closes.

There are posts in between, on Tuesday and Thursday. They explain the method and report where an open call stands. Neither one adds a row to this page.

## The standing calls

Two directional calls run on their own clocks. They are graded separately and they are never counted in the tally. Each one carries the condition that would end it.
